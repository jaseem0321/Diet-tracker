Meal timing guide

Night-shift day

Wake → water/coffee → main meal before shift → planned snack around 10:30 PM–12 AM → second proper meal around 1:30–2:30 AM → light food only if hungry before sleep.

Class + work day

Prioritize sleep whenever possible. Bring a portable protein snack so you don't depend on fast food or sugary drinks.

Friday–Saturday off

Keep meal structure. Enjoy food you like, but think “one flexible meal,” not an unrestricted full cheat day.

Targets
