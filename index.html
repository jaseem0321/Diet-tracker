Night-shift tracking rule

Wake → Start My Day → meals/classes/work → keep logging after midnight → finish shift → final log → End My Day before sleeping.

Example: Start Sunday 4 PM → work 8 PM–5 AM Monday → End Monday morning = one Sunday tracking cycle.

Suggested foods / grocery guide

Budget-friendly choices for a working student. Mix and match—dili kinahanglan paliton tanan.

Protein — priority

Eggs · chicken breast/thigh (skinless when practical) · canned tuna/sardines · tilapia/bangus · lean pork · tofu/tokwa · monggo · milk · plain/Greek yogurt

Carbs / energy

Rice · oats · sweet potato/camote · potatoes · whole-wheat bread · bananas

Vegetables & fruit

Pechay · cabbage · carrots · cucumber · tomatoes · sayote · kangkong · mixed frozen vegetables · bananas · apples · oranges/pomelo · papaya

Easy night-shift snacks

Boiled eggs + banana · tuna sandwich · yogurt + fruit · oats + milk · tofu/tokwa · fruit + milk

Limit / buy less often

Soft drinks and sugary drinks · chips · pastries · frequent fast food · processed meats. You don't need to ban them; keep portions/frequency controlled.

Simple grocery basket

Protein: eggs, chicken, tuna/sardines, tofu/monggo
Staples: rice, oats, bread
Produce: 2–3 affordable vegetables + 2 fruits
Backup snacks: bananas, milk/yogurt

Tip: choose what fits your budget and what you'll actually eat. Protein portions plus vegetables/fruit make meals easier to structure.

Meal timing guide

Night-shift day

Wake → water/coffee → main meal before shift → planned snack around 10:30 PM–12 AM → second proper meal around 1:30–2:30 AM → light food only if hungry before sleep.

Class + work day

Prioritize sleep whenever possible. Bring a portable protein snack so convenience food does not decide your diet.

Friday–Saturday off

Keep meal structure. Enjoy food you like, but think one flexible meal rather than an unrestricted full cheat day.
